The Ladder
of Light

Sixteen stops. From a single atom to everything there is — thirty-seven orders of magnitude, climbed one screen at a time.

Everything you are about to see is drawn by code, from a seed, the same way every time. Nothing here is a photograph. The universe does not need one.

↓

You have climbed thirty-seven orders of magnitude, and nothing up there noticed.

But near the middle of the ladder, on a small planet, a small creature stopped, looked up the rungs — and felt its chest tighten at the size of it all.

That creature is the only known place in the universe where awe happens.

The ladder was never the wonder. The climbing was.
